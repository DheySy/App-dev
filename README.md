# 💪 Strong Woman Do Bong Soon.

**Strong Woman Do Bong Soon** is a South Korean television series that aired in **2017**.
It stars *Park Bo-young*, *Park Hyung-sik*, and *Ji Soo*.

> “Don’t underestimate a small woman with a big punch!”

---

## 📝 Synopsis

Do Bong Soon is a petite woman born with **superhuman strength** that’s been passed down through the women in her family.
She dreams of creating her own video game, but ends up becoming the **bodyguard** of a CEO who recognizes her power and potential.

---

### 🌟 Main Cast

1. **Park Bo-young** as Do Bong Soon
2. **Park Hyung-sik** as Ahn Min-hyuk
3. **Ji Soo** as In Guk-doo
